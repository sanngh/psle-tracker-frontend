Privacy Policy — PSLE Tracker (Closed Beta)

Last updated: 1 September 2026

PSLE Tracker ("the App") is operated by PSLE Tracker Team ("we", "us", "the Beta Administrator"), based in Singapore. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and how it is protected, in accordance with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

The App is currently offered as a free, closed beta to a limited group of parent and student testers, and is not yet publicly available on the Play Store / App Store for general download.

1. What personal data we collect

DataWhy we collect itWhere it is stored
Mobile phone number (parent's, and the child's if they have a separate linked student account)Account login / identity verificationTurso (managed SQLite database)
PIN (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text)App login securityTurso
Practice paper marks entered by the parent/student (subject, score, and completion date for prelims/practice exam papers, plus syllabus/revision progress) — not official school academic recordsCore app function — tracking PSLE practice/revision progressTurso
Uploaded photos of test papers / worksheets ("evidence")Core app function — proof of completed revisionCloudflare R2 (object storage)

We do not collect: your name, your child's full name, email address, school name, NRIC, home address, or any official school academic records/transcripts. The App only stores marks entered for practice/prelim papers the child has completed at home, plus revision progress. Parents/students are responsible for ensuring uploaded photos do not accidentally reveal such details, and should crop/redact where possible.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your login (phone number + PIN)
  • To let you track syllabus coverage, revision progress, and practice/prelim paper marks for your child (not official school records)
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for their own marketing or commercial purposes

3. Where your data is hosted

Your data is processed and stored using the following infrastructure providers, acting solely as data processors on our instructions:

  • Google Cloud Run (Singapore/Asia-Southeast region) — hosts the backend application server
  • Turso (managed libSQL/SQLite database) — stores account and academic records
  • Cloudflare R2 — stores uploaded evidence photos

All data in transit between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

4. Data retention and annual purge

This beta program operates on an annual cycle. On or before 31 December each year, all accounts, phone numbers, academic records, and photos collected during that calendar year are permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers.

5. Your rights under the PDPA

You may, at any time:

  • Withdraw your consent to data collection
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data

To exercise any of these rights, contact the Beta Administrator at singh.iitr2004@gmail.com. Deletion requests are actioned within 30 days.

6. Children's data

The App is used by parents/legal guardians on behalf of their children, or directly by students under parental consent. A parent or legal guardian must provide consent before any student account is activated, and that consent explicitly covers the collection of the child's own mobile phone number (where a separate student account is linked) and the upload of photos of the child's test papers/worksheets for progress tracking. We do not knowingly collect data directly from a child without such consent.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical safeguards, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), hashed credentials (PINs are never stored in plain text), and access-controlled cloud infrastructure, to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be communicated to registered users by email before they take effect.
